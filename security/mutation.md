# Mutation testing (public evidence, MUT-PUBLIC)

Run 2026-11-15 on private dev commit `96d4225` (tag `audit-2026-11-15`); `contracts/src` was unchanged by the run. Rerun with `.github/scripts/mutation.sh` from this tree. The summary is also in `SECURITY.md` (Mutation testing).

## Method

- Mutants: the slither-mutate mutators (slither-analyzer, all 15 operators: RR, CR, ROR, LOR, AOR, ASOR, BOR, UOR,
  LIR, MIA, MVIE, MVIV, MWA, FHR, SBR), asked for their patches directly (`.github/scripts/mutation/gen.py`) so the runs can
  be parallel. 9,020 mutants over the six targets (ODBacker 2,343, ODGovernor 2,285, ODOracleGuard 1,936,
  ODMinter 1,087, ODMarketHours 1,051, ODHook 318). The exit queue lives in ODMinter (`queueSell`, `fillQueued`).
- Sample: 35 mutants per contract, fixed seed 1115 (210 in all), to fit about 2 hours on a shared machine.
- Kill test: the hermetic forge suite (RHC_RPC_URL unset; `test/fork/` and `test/halmos/` not compiled), the suites
  that own each contract (`NARROW` in `.github/scripts/mutation/run.py`): minter + SellQueueInvariant, backer, governor,
  hook + minter/Hook, oracle/OracleGuard + ReopenRound + HolidayReopen, oracle/MarketHours + Calendar + HolidayReopen.
  Fuzz 64 runs, invariant 16 x 16, via_ir kept, optimiser off (same semantics, minutes faster per mutant);
  the `GasBound` tests are skipped because they measure optimised bytecode. 4 parallel workers, 900 s timeout.
- Survivors of the owning suites were then checked against the wider hermetic suites that name the contract
  (poc, audit, system). A full broad re-run was stopped: with the optimiser off two suites (SystemInvariant,
  agent/AgentRouter) hit stack-too-deep, and each broad mutant costs about 4 minutes of compile. The three
  survivors that a wider suite clearly targets were re-run against it and died.
- Uncompiled mutants (solc refused the mutation, mostly RR/SBR on non-void or typed code) are not scored.
  Score = killed / (killed + survived + timed out).
