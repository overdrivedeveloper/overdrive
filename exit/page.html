Overdrive exit page

One file. It runs in your browser and talks only to your own wallet and the Overdrive contracts. It needs no Overdrive website and no Overdrive server. Check the file's hash against the published hash before you use it.

1. Connect

Contracts this page uses

Compare these with the addresses in the published deployment file before you send anything.

2. Sell gears

Gear holders are never liquidated. If the market is closed or there is no fresh price, use Queue sell: your gears wait in escrow and are paid at the next fresh price. You can cancel a queued sell and get the gears back.

3. Your queued sells

A queued sell can be filled by anyone once there is a fresh price at or above your minimum. Press Claim to try to fill it yourself.

4. Governor withdrawals

Your Governor shares: -. A withdrawal is requested first and can be claimed after the delay. You set the smallest amount of USDG you will take when you claim.

5. Backer vaults

Backers are different from gear holders: a vault can be liquidated if its collateral falls short of what its debt needs. Repaying is never blocked. Taking collateral out is allowed only while the vault stays above its required ratio.

This page gives no advice. Every send is simulated first so a refusal is explained in plain words before you sign. Your wallet shows the final transaction: read it.
